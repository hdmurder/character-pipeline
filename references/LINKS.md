# Public implementation and context links

These links are curated from the broader character-training collection. Build artifacts, malformed URLs, private archives, conversations and social provenance are excluded. Bibliographic citations are in [references.bib](references.bib); this page is not an endorsement or license audit.

[Open Character Training](https://github.com/maiush/OpenCharacterTraining) accompanies the constitutional character-training paper. Use the upstream paper's repository link rather than assuming similarly named repositories are authoritative.

[Moving Castles Zero report](https://movingcastles.world/posts/zero) and [model](https://huggingface.co/movingcastles/zero) provide another character/data-generation comparison. Verify exact revisions, licenses and output rights before reuse.

[Computer-10](https://huggingface.co/cosmicoptima/computer-10) is a predecessor reference for the separate Computer-11 experiment, not our student checkpoint or a Golem result.

[CoSER implementation](https://github.com/Neph0s/CoSER) and [Ditto](https://github.com/OFA-Sys/Ditto) are role-playing research implementation leads. Their licenses and data rights must be checked separately.

[constitutional_grafting](https://github.com/wassname/constitutional_grafting), [grafting-beliefs](https://github.com/peternutter/grafting-beliefs) and [Shadow-FT](https://github.com/wutaiqiang/Shadow-FT) are references for paired-checkpoint weight transfer. Our public repository does not yet implement these training integrations.

[Assistant Axis](https://github.com/safety-research/assistant-axis) and [repeng](https://github.com/vgel/repeng) are activation-space research implementation references.

[neuronary](https://neuronary.org/), its [implementation](https://github.com/lyramakesmusic/neuronary), and the [Neuron Persona Library](https://neuronpersonas.com/) are exploratory catalogs. Labels describe outputs, not validated localized identities.

[Golden Gate Claude](https://www.anthropic.com/research/golden-gate-claude) provides a feature-amplification example. Generic concept salience can be absorbed into a first-person identity claim.

[SauersML/watching](https://github.com/SauersML/watching) and [magician](https://github.com/SauersML/magician) motivate checking monitor observability and intervention robustness. Their demonstrations do not establish that a character model can detect all monitoring.

[Google's SLEEP framework](https://research.google/blog/the-sleep-framework-a-new-way-for-llms-to-retain-knowledge-in-continual-learning/) is a continual-learning lead, not part of the completed recipe.

[World of SWECraft project](https://szj.io/sw) supplies collaboration-environment context. [Avalon-M1](https://www.gr.inc/releases/avalon-m1) supplies a collective-training comparison and safety questions.

Source-model references are [Qwen3.5-4B-Base](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B-Base), [Qwen3-30B-A3B](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-30B-A3B) and [d1-2B](https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/d1-2B). These links do not replace immutable revisions or independent artifact verification.
